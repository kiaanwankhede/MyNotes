# BTPAI

Notes for an SAP consultant or developer who starts from zero on SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) and goes on to SAP's AI stack: Joule, Joule Studio, agents and AI Core.

* Source: a live course, *BTP Foundation & SAP Artificial Intelligence Training* (Mentors Pool, batch of 27 Sep 2026): recordings, slides, notes and assignments.
* Every lecture and file is tracked in `master/master.xlsx` with IDs starting `BT`.
* Laid out by concept, in study order, not by lecture:
  * **BTP Foundations:** cloud basics, the SAP landscape, the account model, users and identity
  * **SAP AI:** SAP's AI strategy, platform and careers
  * **AI Fundamentals:** how machine learning and large language models work, and prompting
  * **Joule:** SAP's assistant family, starting with Joule for Consultants
  * **Responsible AI:** ethics, regulation and SAP's AI terms
* New to AI? The AI Fundamentals chapters (13 to 15) can be read before the SAP AI chapters.
* Chapters are added lecture by lecture, as each one is read in full. A later lecture enriches an existing chapter rather than adding a second one on the same idea.
* Screenshots come from the recordings and from public SAP pages. Names, emails and account IDs are pixelated. Slides are redrawn as diagrams or tables, not pasted.
* Anything not taught in the course is labelled **Added by Claude**, and every claim about the current state of an SAP product is web-checked and dated.
